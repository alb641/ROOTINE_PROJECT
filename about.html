Our Origin

The Rootine Story

Rootine was born from personal necessity. At 16, I was diagnosed with Telogen Effluvium, a stress-induced alopecia.

Ever since my diagnosis, keeping stress at bay has been paramount in my routine. However, as I got older, I realized I was still at a disadvantage because I didn't truly understand my hair type or how to care for its unique architecture.

This is a major unsolved problem within black and foreign communities. I leaned into public research to create a platform that allows you to identify your hair type and diagnose its strengths and weaknesses, so you’ll know the best products for your hair type and how to incorporate them into your life.

A Rootine for Everyone

Life stress can cause us to lose track of our self-care. Rootine combats that by establishing a routine that fits perfectly into your schedule, empowering you to reclaim your hair's health through data.
