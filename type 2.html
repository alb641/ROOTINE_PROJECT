TYPE 2: WAVY

The Wavy Architecture

Type 2 hair falls between straight and curly. It has a distinctive "S" pattern and can be prone to frizz. The key to a healthy Type 2 "Rootine" is finding products that enhance the wave without weighing it down with heavy oils.

Recommendation: Use light-weight mousses and sea-salt sprays to maintain volume and definition.
