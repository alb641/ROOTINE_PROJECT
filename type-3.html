TYPE 3: CURLY

The Curly Architecture

Type 3 hair is defined by springy, well-defined curls that can range from loose loops to tight ringlets. This hair type is naturally drier because the natural oils from the scalp have a harder time traveling down the spiral shape of the hair strand.

Recommendation: Prioritize deep conditioning treatments and leave-in conditioners. A creamy "Rootine" will help lock in moisture and maintain your curl pattern throughout the week.
